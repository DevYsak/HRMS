# Pulse v3.1 audit — §3.5 Compensation & Finance, §4 RBAC (Compensation / Documents-Payslips), §5.3, §9 (payslip/upload controls)

Read-only audit of branch `staging` (working tree, 2026-10-04). Every reference was read in code. "UNVERIFIED" means the claim depends on runtime/DB behaviour I could not execute under the read-only rule.

Context that applies to many rows:
- Payroll permissions come from DB roles (`User::hasPermission`, app/Models/User.php:146-226). Seeded defaults are in database/seeders/RolesAndPermissionsSeeder.php (ROLE_DEFAULTS). HR Admin holds `run_payroll` but not `approve_finance`. **Director holds `approve_finance`, `approve_payroll`, `view_payroll`, `view_payslips` and `manage_employees`.** Finance holds `run_payroll`, `approve_finance` and `manage_salary_components`.
- `role:` middleware is persistent for Livewire updates (app/Providers/AppServiceProvider.php:78), so route gates also cover Livewire actions.
- Payroll status machine: `draft → pending_finance → finalized` (migration 2026_04_29_184134). Payslip status: `draft → paid` on finalize (PayrollService.php:381).

## Authorization holes

1. **HR Admin can finalize payroll.** FinanceApproval.php:32-35/46-68/82-115 lets `canRunPayroll()` users approve or reject in the legacy flow. PayrollService::approveFinance (235-252) has no role check. **Fix:** `abort_unless(Auth::user()->canApproveFinance(), 403)` in legacy `approve`/`openReject`/`confirmReject`, and the same check in `approveFinance`/`rejectFinance`. Unused `PayrollPolicy::approveFinance` already encodes this.
2. **HR Admin can remove Finance from the approval chain.** ApprovalPolicySettings.php:64 accepts a chain with no finance step. **Fix:** reject save/submit unless ≥1 active step is `finance` (or a specific user with `canApproveFinance()`).
3. **HR/Finance approve incentives, including their own.** Incentives.php:69-81 and IncentiveService.php:27-56 have no role, status or self guard. They can also reject an already-paid incentive, which nulls `payroll_id`. The Director (spec approver) can't reach the page (web.php:328). **Fix:** Director/SA-only approve/reject, `status==='pending'`, approver ≠ requester ≠ employee.
4. **HR/Finance create and approve reimbursements, including their own, with no manager.** Payroll/Reimbursements.php:62-107 and ReimbursementService.php:29-64 have no status or self guard. **Fix:** pending-only guard + `ApprovalGuard::assertNotSelf`.
5. **Director edits anyone's salary and approves payroll.** EmployeeEdit.php:519-564 authorizes only `update` (= `manage_employees`, held by Director). The Director's `approve_finance` opens FinanceApproval/FinanceDashboard (all salaries) and lets them finalize runs. **Fix:** `abort_unless(Auth::user()->canRunPayroll(), 403)` on salary actions and hide the Pay tab. Removing `approve_finance` from the Director is a **business decision** plus a prod role_permission change.
6. **Historical Import creates a self-approved "finalized" payroll for any period.** HistoricalImport (route run-payroll only) → PayrollHistoricalImportService.php:272-290. **Fix:** period < current month and `canApproveFinance()`.
7. **Payslips editable/deletable while pending Finance.** Any run-payroll user can edit or delete payslips during pending_finance, even after a step was approved (Process.php:303-320, 394-420; PayrollService.php:598-656). **Fix:** non-draft ⇒ require `canApproveFinance()`, reset steps, no delete.
8. **Employees see, download and email unapproved draft payslips.** MyPayslips.php:249,268; PayslipController::download 150-172. **Fix:** `paid` only for non-payroll users.
9. **Self-compensation.** HR/Finance can change their own salary through SalaryStructures::assign (133-160) and EmployeeEdit::saveSalary (531-556). **Fix:** `assertNotSelf`.
10. **Low risk:** `Process::$cycle`/`$month` are unvalidated; tampering creates junk payroll rows (Process.php:197-223). In FinanceEncashments.php:74-77 a Finance user can reject an HR-stage request by tampering `reviewingId`.

No payslip IDOR found: owner-or-`canRunPayroll` is enforced on single, combined and bulk downloads.
